About Me

Researching Secure and Decentralized Systems

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at New Mexico State University. My research focuses on security and privacy, distributed systems, blockchain LLMs, and Machine learning.

My work investigates how decentralized infrastructures can remain secure, scalable, fair, and resilient under adversarial conditions.

I am particularly interested in practical blockchain experimentation, Ethereum, transaction processing, cryptographic protocols, and security evaluation.
